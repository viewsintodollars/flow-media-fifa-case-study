01 · Our role

We were the production partner.

Unite8 Sports ran World Cup coverage on Instagram for Indian fans, alongside the live stream on ZEE5. The page had to stay live through every matchday for 45 days, not just the big fixtures.

  • Post-production for the full tournament, 1 June to 19 July 2026
  • Match moments turned around on matchdays, while the conversation was still live
  • Reaction cuts, trend edits and memes, made for Instagram rather than lifted from TV
  • Edited for an Indian audience: local references, local humour

05 · What made the edits work

Cut for Instagram, cut for India.

01

Feeling first

We opened on the reaction, the tears, the celebration. The scoreline came second.

02

Native, not a TV replay

Fast cuts, reaction edits, memes and trend formats. Edits made for the feed did better than broadcast clips.

03

Made in India, for India

Familiar faces like Sandesh Jhingan, local humour, Indian internet references. A global event, cut for the people watching it here.

04

A system, not a lucky post

Around 25 posts a day kept every matchday live. The growth came from the process, not one viral spike.
